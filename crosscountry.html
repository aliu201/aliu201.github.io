My Basketball Journey

Basketball has been a big part of my life since elementary school. I started playing with the SK Knights Youth Team and later joined KISAC, where I made many friends and learned the value of teamwork. I practice almost every evening, whether at the gym or on outdoor courts, and I even attended a basketball camp in Australia. One of my favorite memories is traveling with my family to New York to watch NBA games, which inspired me to keep improving. Basketball is not just a sport for me, it is a way to challenge myself, connect with others, and grow stronger both on and off the court.

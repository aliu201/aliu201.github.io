  4. Sep 2025 HOBY Korea International Young Scholars Conference Seoul, South Korea

    First author

    Social Media Usage and Literacy

    • Published in Knowledge to Lead (Vol. 6, Issue 1), the official journal of HOBY Korea, which carries selected conference submissions.
    • Presented the findings at the international conference and was evaluated positively on analytical rigour, methodological depth and clarity.
    • Collected and compared historical datasets spanning 20 years of social-media expansion against 150 years of literacy development to test for a causal relationship.
    • Used AI-assisted data visualisation to show that literacy rates have risen independently of social-media growth — which cuts against the usual assumption about digital influence.
    • Conducted independently under the mentorship of Ivy League professors and PhD researchers.
